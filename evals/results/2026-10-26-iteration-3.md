## Notes

- **Size went up.** The new designs are 1,692 and 1,768 words against 1,393 and 1,507. The judge finds fewer wasted paragraphs in them, so the growth is reasons
  and consequences, not restatement; but the skill has no size rule and the user's complaint was size. Watch `perf.words` on the production designs.
- **The seven questions became the headings** in both new runs (Problem, Cause, Approach, Structure, Rules, What changes for callers, Left out, Open). A de
  facto template, which is fine as long as nothing is written to fill one.
- **No design in either variant captions its diagram.** All four fail the `title` item of `diagram-stands-alone`; `views.md` asks for a caption line before the
  diagram and the runs put the diagram under a heading instead. A wording fix for the next iteration.
- **The builder check ran** in both new runs: a fresh subagent found two or three minor points each time, settled and marked as the author's call.
- **A run reported a conflict:** `writing-adr` said it is fired after design approval while brainstorming commits the `proposed` ADR with the design. Fixed in
  the same iteration: writing-adr is fired once the approach is chosen.
- **What this loop cannot show.** A five-line brief on a small Python fixture has no story gaps, no coexistence, no domain vocabulary and no
  `.claude/designs.md`; the subagent answered its own questions, so dialogue quality (one question at a time, options with costs) is visible only in the
  transcripts. Two reps are barely a noise floor. The decision rests on the next production designs judged as variant `v1` against `evals/private/runs/asa`.
